! omarchy-qutebrowser — tracker & telemetry blocking (our own, always-on)
!
! Request-level companion to the upstream lists. Lives in the repo for the same reason
! as youtube-ads.txt: the most common trackers are blocked from this list alone, so
! blocking does not depend on an upstream list being current (or, offline, on it being
! cached at all). Upstream lists still add breadth and site-specific rules.
!
! Syntax is ABP/adblock, parsed by Brave's adblock-rust via qutebrowser.
!   ||domain^   block the domain and all its subdomains
! Keep it to unambiguous trackers/telemetry: `unbreak.txt` (already in the list set)
! and per-site whitelists handle the rare site that needs one of these to function.

! omarchy-qutebrowser — video ad blocking (YouTube pre-roll, mid-roll, overlay)
!
! Request-level companion to userscripts/youtube-adblock.js. qutebrowser's blocker
! can only filter requests, so this list kills the ad endpoints; everything that
! has to happen inside the page (hiding ad slots, fast-forwarding an ad that still
! plays, and stripping adParams from the player request) is done by the userscript.
!
! Deliberately NOT blocked: googlevideo.com — that is the video CDN itself, and
! blocking it takes the actual video with it.

! ── YouTube ad endpoints ──
||youtube.com/pagead/
||youtube.com/ptracking
||youtube.com/api/stats/ads
||youtube.com/get_midroll_info
||youtube.com/pcs/activeview
||ads.youtube.com^
||youtube.com/youtubei/v1/player/ad_break

! ── Google ad delivery (page + in-video) ──
||googlesyndication.com^
||doubleclick.net^
||googleadservices.com^
||googleads.g.doubleclick.net^
||static.doubleclick.net^
||2mdn.net^
||googletagservices.com^
||google.com/pagead/
